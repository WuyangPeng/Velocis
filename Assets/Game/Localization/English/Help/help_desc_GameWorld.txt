"Nine Provinces" is a game set in the chaotic times of ancient Chinese history, with role-playing (RPG) as its core, integrating elements of strategic conflict and historical simulation.

In the game, you will play as a "Lord" in chaotic times, experiencing the grand era from a personal narrative perspective. Unlike cold digital cards, the legendary generals in the game possess independent personality traits, personal ideals, and dynamic social networks. Through rich story exploration, exclusive general biographies, and profound fate choices, you will build deep "Lord-General Bonds" with your followers.

While featuring strategy elements like territory management, diplomacy, and sandtable battles, the game emphasizes the immersive RPG experience of "Lord and generals united as one to conquer the realm." Every choice you make will shape your benevolent or dominant personality, affect the loyalty and defection of various generals, and ultimately rewrite the epic of the Nine Provinces.
